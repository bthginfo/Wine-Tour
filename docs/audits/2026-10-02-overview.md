## Release approach

Implementations are coordinated by file ownership. Review uses isolated local account/state fixtures, not production records. A combined build and targeted independent browser evaluation are required before pushing. Production persistence, upload authorization and multi-user synchronization are outside what these local fixtures can prove.

## Content quality principle

Missing evidence must not be replaced by plausible-sounding local history, estate philosophy, legal blend rules or bottle-specific cellar claims. General wine education and an individual record's verified properties are different things. Empty unsupported fields should not generate empty presentation cards.

The catalog currently contains 222 regions, 134 varieties, 347 producers and 601 wines. The audit identifies uneven evidence coverage: 34 region terroir profiles, 68 variety botanical/photo references and 83 producer-specific wine records. These counts describe the available evidence categories, not a score for the truth or completeness of every record. The remaining content requires further record-specific research; adding prose alone does not close that gap.

## Verification

Release checks and independent review outcome are recorded after integration in the browser report. No claim of complete production end-to-end verification is made.
