# Content systems audit — 2 October 2026

## Scope and limits

Reviewed the shared content paths for regional profiles, grape profiles, wine and producer records, lessons, interactive learning patterns, and reference guides, including their EN/DE/FR/ES fallback generation. This is a source-level audit of the catalog and generation logic, not a claim that every fact in the catalog has been independently researched. The catalog validators were run for the baseline inventory before the final copy patch; the integrated release build and final post-patch validation remain with the root task.

|---|---:|---:|---|

The initial catalog and learning validator runs passed. They reported 222 regions, 134 grapes, 347 producers, 601 wines, 77 aromas, 9 classic blends, 74 source domains, 11 authored modules, 27 reference guides, 8 schools, 10 archetypes, and 68 verified botanical/photo pairs. Those checks validate structure and coverage rules; they do not establish claim-by-claim factual accuracy.

## System findings and changes

### Regions

The previous fallback made every region read as if it had researched local history, seasonal timing, viticulture, wine styles and pairings. It also dressed generic teaching copy up as region-specific evidence. For the 188 entries without a regional terroir profile, the fallback now states only the linked geography and variety relationships; climate, soil, history, growing season, viticulture, styles, pairings and key facts are left empty. For the 34 profile entries, climate and soil are presented as broad notes, with explicit caution that site conditions vary; unsupported history and practice copy is cleared. The UI owner is hiding empty sections.

Two region-specific distinctions were checked against primary sources and added in all four locales:

- Nemea: the geographic growing area is not interchangeable with its PDO rules. Red PDO Nemea is 100% Agiorgitiko; the 2020 Domaine Skouras Megas Oenos technical sheet identifies the wine as PGI Peloponnese despite listing vineyards in Nemea. Sources: [Wines of Greece — PDO Nemea](https://winesofgreece.org/pdo/pdo-nemea/) and [Domaine Skouras — Megas Oenos 2020 technical sheet](https://skouras.gr/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/MEGASOINOS-RED-US-1.pdf).
- Mosel: the profile now distinguishes soil descriptions by valley section instead of presenting slate, limestone, clay and alluvium as interchangeable generic regional cards. Source: [Wines of Germany — Mosel](https://www.winesofgermany.com/our-regions/growing-area/72/mosel).

The catalog has source URLs associated with regions, but a regional or national URL is not necessarily evidence for every climate, soil or history sentence. Per-claim provenance remains a substantial gap.

### Grapes

The former fallback turned heuristic acidity/tannin/body scores into categorical prose, then used those same scores to generate climate advice, cellar claims, styles and pairings. Those are not supported by PlantGrape morphology records. The profile now retains explicit origin entries, non-default ripening descriptions and direct botanical/growth/susceptibility notes when a PlantGrape record exists. The generic default origin and default mid-season wording are suppressed. Heuristic numeric structure summaries, climate-fit claims, generic winemaking, styles and pairings are cleared unless a catalog translation explicitly supplies a replacement. The catalog score fields themselves remain in the data model and may still influence other learning interactions; they should not be treated as sourced measurements.

### Producers

The non-authored producer fallback previously converted a region relationship into a claim about each estate's philosophy, vineyard work, cellar practice and speciality. Those fields are now empty for non-authored records; a concise listing summary remains. The 24 authored multilingual producer profiles keep their localized authored material. Region-level evidence is no longer substituted for estate-level evidence.

### Wines

The style-context fallback previously generated bottle-specific claims about composition, vinification, maturation, drinking windows and food pairings. Those fields are now empty for style-context wines. Their concise summary identifies the record as a linked wine/style entry and names linked varieties without implying proportions; the serving field is kept as general style guidance. Producer-specific authored material is preserved for records marked at producer evidence level. Because 444 records resolve only to a regional source, this remains an orientation catalog rather than a fully researched bottle catalog.

### Lessons and reference guides

Every learning archetype previously rendered `comparison-lab` and `simulator` from the same control label and three states, so the page repeated the same exercise under two names. The comparison contract now has per-archetype localized conditions, a focused prompt and separate feedback outcomes; the simulator remains the three-state control. Four sensory exercises now have their own localized choice/observation contracts rather than reusing simulator outcomes. The interaction owner is rendering these contracts.

The 11 authored modules and 27 reference guides provide substantially more reusable instruction than the catalog fallbacks, but their source lists and authoring status should not be confused with claim-level citations for every referenced region, producer or bottle. Keep general wine education clearly distinct from record-specific claims.

## Remaining risks / next research

- Research regional climate and soil notes with regional sources and attach provenance to individual claims; 34/222 regional profiles are not enough to generalize across the atlas.
- Do not equate 68/134 botanical/photo evidence pairs with researched sensory profiles. Variety acidity, tannin, body, styles and pairings still need dedicated evidence or explicit authored editorial review.
- Expand producer-specific coverage beyond 24 authored profiles and wine-specific evidence beyond 83/601 records. A populated region link or a winery name is not a substitute for producer/product documentation.
- Audit guide and lesson claims against their cited technical sources during future editorial work; structural validators do not perform this review.
- Preserve the empty-field UI guards: cleared fields must not reappear as empty cards or sections.
